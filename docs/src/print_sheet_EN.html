🥑 AvocadoBook toy laptop – print sheet (Anycubic Kobra X + ACE)

Starting values: Anycubic does not publish a dedicated PETG profile for the Kobra X. Start from the Kobra X + PETG profile of Anycubic Slicer Next (version ≥ 1.3.9.3) and change only the items below. Speeds not listed, pressure advance and retraction: keep the profile values. Everything below is already set in the project file AvocadoBook_progetto.3mf.

Every 3MF has one group per material ("1 GRIGIO PETG" = grey, "2 BIANCO PETG" = white, "3 NERO PETG" = black, "4 PLA (supporti)" = PLA supports): assign the filament to the group, not to single parts. On the printer, the type of each slot must match the slicer (PETG/PLA). Spools without RFID: set type and colour of the slot by hand.

5. Assembly (summary – see the assembly instructions)

  1. In the bottom, push the 10 spring strips upright into their slots, rail down: one front and one back strip per row (the back one is mirrored). The file name gives row and side (fila 1 = number row; davanti = front, dietro = back).
  2. Place every key: its 4 tabs slide into the channels of the ribs. Trackpad: insert the 2 short strips, then place the trackpad.
  3. Lid: slide the hinge axle from the front into the two cradles of the bottom and open it to at least 105° (at 90° the screen gets in the way of the top case).
  4. Lower the top case from above: the two hinge covers go over the axle. Press at the back first, then towards the front, until all hooks click.
